Quick Decision Tools

ICU / ER / Critical Care

Ventilator Initial Settings

Mode AC/VC or PC
Tidal Volume 6-8 mL/kg IBW
RR 12-20 /min
FiO₂ Start 100% → wean
PEEP 5-8 cmH₂O
I:E Ratio 1:2
Plateau P <30 cmH₂O
ARDS: TV 4-6 mL/kg, PEEP per ARDSNet table, Pplat ≤30

Vasopressors & Inotropes

Norepinephrine 0.01-3 µg/kg/min
Vasopressin 0.03-0.04 U/min
Epinephrine 0.01-0.5 µg/kg/min
Dobutamine 2.5-20 µg/kg/min
Dopamine 2-20 µg/kg/min
Phenylephrine 0.5-9 µg/kg/min
1st line septic shock: Norepinephrine. Add Vasopressin at NE >0.25 µg/kg/min

Sedation & Analgesia

Propofol 5-80 µg/kg/min
Dexmedetomidine 0.2-1.5 µg/kg/hr
Midazolam 0.02-0.1 mg/kg/hr
Fentanyl 25-200 µg/hr
Morphine 2-30 mg/hr
Target RASS: -2 to 0. Analgesia first (A1) strategy. Daily SAT+SBT.

Electrolyte Correction

K⁺ <3.5: 10 mEq KCl raises K⁺ by ~0.1. Max 20 mEq/hr peripheral, 40 mEq/hr central.
Mg²⁺ <1.6: 2g MgSO₄ IV over 1hr. Correct Mg before K.
Na⁺ correction: Max 8 mEq/L per 24hrs (risk ODS). 3% NaCl for severe (<120).
PO₄³⁻ <2.0: K-Phos or Na-Phos 15-30 mmol IV over 4-6hrs.
Ca²⁺ <7.0: 1-2g Ca gluconate IV over 10-20 min. Monitor QTc.

ABG Quick Interpretation

pH7.35 - 7.45
PaCO₂35 - 45 mmHg
HCO₃⁻22 - 26 mEq/L
PaO₂80 - 100 mmHg
AG8 - 12 mEq/L
Steps: 1) Acidemia/Alkalemia? 2) Respiratory or Metabolic? 3) Compensation? 4) AG? 5) Delta-Delta

RSI Checklist

PPreparation (equipment, team, plan A/B/C)
PPreoxygenation (3 min NRB / HFNC 60L / NIV)
PPretreatment (Fentanyl 1-3µg/kg if ↑ICP)
PParalysis + Induction (Ketamine 1-2mg/kg + Roc 1.2mg/kg)
PPlacement (confirm EtCO₂ waveform)
PPost-intubation (CXR, set vent, sedation)

Cardiac Arrest (ACLS)

Confirm Cardiac Arrest
Unresponsive + No pulse (10 sec max check)
Start CPR + Attach Defibrillator
30:2 or continuous if advanced airway. Rate 100-120/min, depth 5-6cm
Shockable? (VF/pVT)
YES → Shock 200J biphasic → CPR 2min
Non-Shockable? (PEA/Asystole)
Epinephrine 1mg IV q3-5min immediately. Identify reversible cause (Hs & Ts)
After 2nd Shock (if VF/pVT)
Epinephrine 1mg + Amiodarone 300mg IV push
ROSC?
12-lead ECG, targeted temp management 32-36°C, PCI if STEMI
Hs: Hypovolemia, Hypoxia, H⁺, Hypo/Hyperkalemia, Hypothermia
Ts: Tension pneumo, Tamponade, Toxins, Thrombosis (PE/MI)

Anaphylaxis

Recognize
Urticaria + Hypotension + Bronchospasm + Angioedema (any 2 systems)
Epinephrine IM (Anterolateral Thigh)
Adult: 0.3-0.5 mg (1:1000) IM. Repeat q5-15min
Position + Access
Supine with legs elevated. 2 large bore IVs. NS bolus 1-2L
Adjuncts
Albuterol nebulizer, Diphenhydramine 50mg IV, Methylpred 125mg IV
Refractory?
Epinephrine IV infusion 0.1-1 µg/kg/min. Glucagon if on β-blockers (1-5mg IV)

Sepsis / Septic Shock (Hour-1 Bundle)

qSOFA ≥2 or Suspected Infection
RR ≥22, Altered mentation, SBP ≤100
Measure Lactate
If >2 mmol/L → remeasure within 2-4 hrs
Blood Cultures × 2 Before Antibiotics
Do NOT delay antibiotics >1 hour for cultures
Broad-Spectrum Antibiotics Within 1 Hour
Cover gram +/-, consider MRSA, pseudomonas, fungal per risk
30 mL/kg Crystalloid for Hypotension or Lactate ≥4
Reassess volume status with dynamic parameters (PLR, PPV)
Vasopressors if MAP <65 Despite Fluids
Norepinephrine 1st line. Target MAP ≥65. Add Vasopressin 2nd.

Status Epilepticus

0-5 min: Stabilize
ABCs, O₂, glucose check, IV access, vitals
5-20 min: 1st Line Benzodiazepine
Lorazepam 0.1 mg/kg IV (max 4mg/dose) OR Midazolam 10mg IM
20-40 min: 2nd Line AED
Levetiracetam 60mg/kg (max 4500mg) IV OR Fosphenytoin 20mg PE/kg OR Valproate 40mg/kg
>40 min: Refractory → RSI + Infusion
Midazolam / Propofol / Pentobarbital infusion. Continuous EEG. ICU.

DKA Management Algorithm

Diagnosis: pH <7.3, HCO₃ <18, Glucose >250, Ketones +, AG >12
1. Fluids
NS 1-1.5 L/hr × 1hr → 250-500 mL/hr. Switch to D5½NS when glucose <200
2. Insulin
Regular insulin 0.1-0.14 U/kg/hr IV. Target glucose drop 50-70 mg/dL/hr
3. Potassium
K⁺ <3.3 → hold insulin, replace 20-40 mEq/hr. K⁺ 3.3-5.3 → add 20-30 mEq/L. K⁺ >5.3 → recheck q2h
4. Bicarb (only if pH <6.9)
NaHCO₃ 100 mEq in 400 mL over 2hrs
Resolution: AG <12, pH >7.3, HCO₃ ≥15, glucose <200. Overlap SC insulin 2hr before stopping drip.

STEMI Algorithm

STEMI Criteria: ≥1mm ST↑ in 2 contiguous leads (≥2mm in V1-V3 men, ≥1.5mm women) OR new LBBB
Immediate (MONA-B)
Morphine (if pain), O₂ (if SpO₂ <90%), Nitro SL (not in RV infarct), Aspirin 325mg, β-blocker (if no CI)
Anticoagulation
Heparin 60U/kg bolus (max 4000U) + 12U/kg/hr. P2Y12 inhibitor (Ticagrelor 180mg or Clopidogrel 600mg)
Reperfusion (Door-to-Balloon <90 min)
Primary PCI preferred. If >120 min to PCI → Fibrinolysis (tPA/TNK) within 30 min door-to-needle

Acute Ischemic Stroke

Time is Brain: Last known well time is KEY. CT head STAT to r/o hemorrhage.
IV tPA (Alteplase) — within 4.5 hrs
0.9 mg/kg (max 90mg). 10% bolus, 90% infusion over 60 min. BP <185/110 before, <180/105 after.
Thrombectomy — within 24 hrs (select patients)
LVO (ICA/M1/M2), NIHSS ≥6, ASPECTS ≥6, favorable perfusion mismatch
Exclusions for tPA
Active bleed, platelets <100K, INR >1.7, recent surgery <14d, BP uncontrolled

Upper GI Bleed Algorithm

Resuscitate
2 large bore IVs, crystalloid, type & crossmatch. Transfuse if Hb <7 (threshold 8 if cardiac). Correct coagulopathy.
Risk Stratify (Glasgow-Blatchford)
Score 0 = safe for outpatient. Score ≥6 = high risk → ICU.
Pharmacotherapy
PPI 80mg IV bolus + 8mg/hr infusion. Erythromycin 250mg IV (prokinetic pre-scope). Octreotide if variceal.
Endoscopy within 24 hrs (12 hrs if high risk)
Forrest Ia/Ib → dual therapy (epi + clip/thermal). Variceal → band ligation + Terlipressin/Octreotide.

Chest Pain Red Flags

  • Tearing/ripping pain radiating to back (Aortic Dissection)
  • New ST elevation or dynamic ECG changes
  • Hypotension + JVD + muffled heart sounds (Beck's Triad → Tamponade)
  • Unilateral absent breath sounds + tracheal deviation (Tension Pneumo)
  • Pleuritic pain + hemoptysis + recent immobilization (PE)
  • Subcutaneous emphysema (Boerhaave/Esophageal rupture)

Headache Red Flags (SNOOP)

  • Systemic symptoms (fever, weight loss, HIV, cancer)
  • Neurological signs (focal deficit, papilledema, seizure)
  • Onset sudden "thunderclap" (SAH until proven otherwise)
  • Older age new onset (>50 → GCA, malignancy)
  • Positional / Progressive / Precipitated by Valsalva
  • "Worst headache of my life" → CT head + LP

Abdominal Pain Red Flags

  • Peritoneal signs (rigidity, guarding, rebound)
  • Hemodynamic instability with abdominal pain (ruptured AAA/ectopic)
  • Pain out of proportion to exam (Mesenteric ischemia)
  • Free air under diaphragm (Hollow viscus perforation)
  • Obstructive pattern + bilious vomiting + no flatus
  • Grey Turner/Cullen sign (retroperitoneal hemorrhage)

Back Pain Red Flags

  • Saddle anesthesia + urinary retention (Cauda Equina → MRI STAT)
  • Progressive bilateral leg weakness
  • History of cancer + new back pain (spinal mets)
  • Fever + IVDU + back pain (Epidural abscess)
  • Age >50 + new onset + unexplained weight loss
  • Pulsatile abdominal mass + back pain (AAA rupture)

Dyspnea Red Flags

  • Stridor (impending airway obstruction → prepare RSI)
  • Silent chest in asthma (near-fatal, exhausted patient)
  • SpO₂ <90% despite high-flow O₂
  • Tripod position + accessory muscle use + inability to speak
  • Acute onset + unilateral pleuritic pain + tachycardia (PE)
  • Frothy pink sputum (Flash pulmonary edema)

Altered Mental Status Red Flags

  • Unequal pupils + GCS drop (herniation → mannitol + neurosurg)
  • Fever + nuchal rigidity + AMS (Meningitis → empiric abx STAT)
  • Focal neuro deficit + acute AMS (Stroke → CT STAT)
  • Hypoglycemia (<60 mg/dL) → Dextrose 50% IV push
  • Pinpoint pupils + respiratory depression (Opioid OD → Naloxone)
  • GCS ≤8 → Secure airway (intubate)

Sepsis & Septic Shock

DO
  • • Antibiotics within 1 hour
  • • Lactate q2-4h to guide therapy
  • • 30 mL/kg bolus early
  • • Norepinephrine as 1st vasopressor
  • • Daily assess for de-escalation
  • • Source control within 6-12 hrs
DON'T
  • • Delay antibiotics for cultures
  • • Use Dopamine 1st line (↑arrhythmia)
  • • Overshoot fluids (lung edema)
  • • Ignore lactate clearance
  • • Steroids unless refractory shock
  • • Forget to reassess volume status

Mechanical Ventilation

DO
  • • Use IBW for tidal volume
  • • Keep Pplat ≤30 cmH₂O
  • • Head of bed 30-45°
  • • Daily SAT + SBT
  • • Lung-protective ventilation in ARDS
  • • Prone if P/F <150
DON'T
  • • Use actual BW for TV (obesity)
  • • Hyperventilate (↓cerebral perfusion)
  • • Allow auto-PEEP in COPD
  • • Deep sedation without reason
  • • Ignore ventilator asynchrony
  • • Forget cuff pressure check (20-30)

Airway Management

DO
  • • Preoxygenate 3+ min (apneic oxygenation HFNC)
  • • Have Plan B/C ready
  • • Confirm placement by EtCO₂
  • • Ramped/sniffing position
  • • Post-intubation CXR
  • • Ketamine if hemodynamically unstable
DON'T
  • • Rush without equipment check
  • • Thiopental/Propofol in shock
  • • Forget post-intubation sedation
  • • Rely solely on auscultation
  • • Multiple attempts without rescue plan
  • • Succinylcholine in hyperkalemia/burns

IV Fluids & Resuscitation

DO
  • • Balanced crystalloids (LR/Plasmalyte)
  • • Reassess after each bolus
  • • Use dynamic parameters (PLR, IVC, SVV)
  • • Blood products in hemorrhagic shock (1:1:1)
  • • Consider albumin in cirrhosis
  • • Stop fluids when no longer responsive
DON'T
  • • NS in large volumes (HCMA risk)
  • • Colloids (HES) → renal injury
  • • Fluids in cardiogenic shock
  • • Ignore fluid overload signs
  • • LR in hyperkalemia
  • • Rely on CVP alone for volume

Cardiac Arrest & CPR

DO
  • • High-quality CPR (rate 100-120, depth 5-6cm)
  • • Minimize interruptions (<10 sec)
  • • Epi q3-5 min in non-shockable
  • • Defibrillate immediately when indicated
  • • EtCO₂ monitoring (quality marker)
  • • Targeted temperature management post-ROSC
DON'T
  • • Pause CPR for intubation >10 sec
  • • Lean on chest between compressions
  • • Shock asystole/PEA
  • • Skip reversible causes (Hs & Ts)
  • • Hyperventilate (↓venous return)
  • • Give Epi before 2nd shock in VF/pVT

ICU Medication Safety

DO
  • • Renal-dose adjust (Vanc, Aminoglycosides)
  • • DVT prophylaxis daily
  • • Stress ulcer ppx if indicated
  • • Glycemic control (140-180 target)
  • • Review all infusions q shift
  • • Taper sedation before stopping
DON'T
  • • Push KCl undiluted (cardiac arrest)
  • • Mix insulin with other infusions
  • • Propofol >5 mg/kg/hr >48h (PRIS)
  • • Nephrotoxics without monitoring
  • • Forget to adjust for hepatic failure
  • • IV push phenytoin in dextrose (precipitates)

For educational reference only. Always follow institutional protocols and clinical judgment.
