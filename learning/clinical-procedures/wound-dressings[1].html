What is a Wound Dressing?

A wound dressing is a sterile pad or covering applied to a wound to promote healing, prevent infection, absorb exudate, and provide an optimal moist wound healing environment. The selection depends on wound type, depth, exudate level, and healing phase.

Primary Purposes of Dressing

Protection

From bacteria & trauma

Moisture

Optimal moist environment

Absorption

Exudate management

Haemostasis

Bleeding control

Wound Healing Phases & Dressing Role

1. Haemostasis

Minutes – Hours

Clot formation, vasoconstriction. Use haemostatic dressings, pressure dressings.

2. Inflammatory

1 – 4 Days

Phagocytosis, debridement. Use antimicrobial, alginate, hydrogel dressings.

3. Proliferative

4 – 21 Days

Granulation, angiogenesis. Use foam, hydrocolloid, collagen dressings.

4. Remodelling

21 Days – 2 Years

Collagen maturation, scar formation. Use silicone sheets, film dressings.

Classification of Dressings

Layers of a Closed Dressing

1

Contact / Primary Layer

Directly on wound. Non-adherent (e.g., paraffin gauze, Mepitel). Prevents granulation tissue disruption.

2

Absorbent / Secondary Layer

Absorbs exudate (gauze pads, foam, alginate). Prevents maceration & strike-through.

3

Padding Layer

Cotton wool / orthopaedic wadding. Cushioning, even pressure distribution, thermal insulation.

4

Outer / Retention Layer

Bandage / tape / tubular net. Holds everything in place. Provides compression if needed.

Step-by-Step Dressing Technique

Bandaging Techniques (Visual)

Simple Spiral

Uniform limb. Each turn overlaps ⅔ of previous.

Spiral Reverse

Conical limb (forearm/leg). Fold back at each turn.

Figure-of-Eight

Joints (elbow, knee, ankle). Allows movement.

Recurrent

Stump / scalp. Back-and-forth over tip, secured with circular turns.

Circular / Roller

Uniform areas. Each turn directly over previous. Anchoring turns.

Buddy Taping

Finger/toe fractures. Adjacent digit as splint.

Wound Colour Classification (TIME Framework)

Black

Necrotic / Eschar

Needs debridement. Hydrogel, enzymatic agents, surgical debridement. Autolytic with occlusive.

Yellow

Sloughy

Fibrinous tissue. Hydrogel, alginate, honey, hydrofiber. May need antimicrobial if infected.

Red

Granulating

Healthy healing. Foam, hydrocolloid, film. Protect & maintain moisture. Non-adherent primary.

Pink

Epithelialising

Final healing. Film, thin hydrocolloid, silicone. Minimal intervention. Protect new tissue.
